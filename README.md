## 🔍 Core Capabilities

### 1. Linguistic Pattern Interceptor
The SSA scans incoming text or live speech transcriptions for 47 distinct manipulation archetypes, including:
- **Nebulous Validation** ("You're not like other girls/guys")
- **Future Faking** (Immediate grand promises without timeline boundaries)
- **Preemptive Scarcity** ("I'm usually busy, but…")
- **Negging 2.0** (Backhanded compliments wrapped in algorithmic timing)
- **Trauma Bonding Triggers** (Rapid emotional vulnerability demands)

Each flag comes with a **deflection script** in 12 languages, designed to neutralize the manipulation without escalating conflict.

### 2. Real-Time Confidence Thermometer
Using a proprietary tonality analysis engine, the SSA measures the emotional temperature of a conversation. It alerts you when:
- Your compliance level is rising due to peer pressure
- The other party is using pace acceleration (speeding up to fatigue your judgment)
- You are being mirror-matched to lower your guard

The output is a simple **green/yellow/red** gauge, along with a suggested verbal deceleration tactic.

### 3. The "Social Hologram" Review Mode
After any interaction (or pasted chat log), the SSA generates a **interaction post-mortem**. This visual timeline shows:
- Moment-by-moment power shifts
- Where you lost grounding
- Which PUA tactic was deployed, and at what exact second
- A strategic counter-factual analysis ("If you had said X here, the outcome changes to Y")

This transforms every awkward encounter into a training module for future resilience.

### 4. Adaptive Defense Matrix Generator
Unlike static scripts, the SSA learns your communication style over time. It builds a personal behavioral baseline and then generates **custom comeback architectures**—not one-liners, but conversation trees that account for multiple response branches.

## ⚙️ How the Detection Engine Works (Conceptual)

The engine is a hybrid of **probabilistic language modeling** and **intent-based behavioral heuristics**.

1. **Signal Extraction:** Incoming message is tokenized into micro-fragments.
2. **Tactic Vector Mapping:** Each fragment is compared against a multidimensional array of rhetorical stratagems. The system does not look for keywords (that's amateur); it looks for *structural signatures*—the way a sentence bends time, assigns blame, or creates false urgency.
3. **Contextual Anchoring:** The engine considers the relationship history (if provided) and the power dynamic (formal vs. informal) to adjust the threat level.
4. **Anti-Anti-Detection Measures:** The 2026 system is trained to spot "stealth" PUA tactics that attempt to mimic vulnerability, such as the "Strategic Vulnerability" reverse-manipulation.
